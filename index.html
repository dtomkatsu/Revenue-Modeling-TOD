How much does it cost to maintain our neighborhoods?

Honolulu spends a massive amount of money each year keeping its roads, water lines, and sewer pipes in working order. Less-dense neighborhoods cost far more per acre to serve than the property tax they pay into the system. Compact, walkable areas often cover their share — and then some. Walk the Skyline corridor station by station to see who pays for whom.
